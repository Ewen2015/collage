# Design rationale

**Theme:** 静电花园
**Pairing sentence:** A garden meets radio static, suggesting nature that remembers a signal.
**Strategy:** surrealism — see `references/movements.md`.
**Format:** poster (1200 × 1500)
**Paper stock:** `cold`. **Inks:** #16283a, #d94f36, #4a86a8, #f0c94b.

## From text to image

- **leaf** (`fern leaf botanical`, role: hero) — the growing thing
- **wave** (`radio waveform oscilloscope`, role: satellite) — the remembered signal

## Composition decisions

- 6 pasted pieces; hero imagery first, then medium scraps, then type, then tape and pencil marks.
- Mixed edges (straight cuts against torn strips) to keep it reading as assemblage rather than a filtered photo.
- Rotations are small and non-repeating; only the two largest pieces carry drop shadow.
- Exposed paper is intentional negative space; exact placement comes from `references/composition.md`.
