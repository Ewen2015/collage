# Design rationale

**Theme:** 夜行列车
**Pairing sentence:** A night train meets an unopened letter, suggesting a departure that never quite happened.
**Strategy:** dada — see `references/movements.md`.
**Format:** poster (1200 × 1500)
**Paper stock:** `newsprint`. **Inks:** #26241f, #b23a2c, #3a5f8a, #c9962e.

## From text to image

- **rail** (`railway track night`, role: hero) — the departure
- **letter** (`old envelope letter paper`, role: ground) — the unsent words

## Composition decisions

- 7 pasted pieces; hero imagery first, then medium scraps, then type, then tape and pencil marks.
- Mixed edges (straight cuts against torn strips) to keep it reading as assemblage rather than a filtered photo.
- Rotations are small and non-repeating; only the two largest pieces carry drop shadow.
- Exposed paper is intentional negative space; exact placement comes from `references/composition.md`.
